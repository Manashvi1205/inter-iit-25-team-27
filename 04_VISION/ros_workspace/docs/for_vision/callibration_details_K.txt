### PROCESS FOR SWITCHING OFF AUTOFOCUS
v4l2-ctl --list-devices
    Hy-13M（8658) -Camera: Hy-13M8 (usb-3610000.usb-2.1):
        /dev/video0
        /dev/video1
        /dev/media1

v4l2-ctl -d /dev/video0 -l
     focus_absolute 0x009a090a (int)    : min=1 max=1023 step=1 default=200 value=200
     focus_automatic_continuous 0x009a090c (bool)   : default=1 value=0

Focus camera at expected distance
v4l2-ctl -d /dev/video0 --set-ctrl=focus_automatic_continuous=0
v4l2-ctl -d /dev/video0 --set-ctrl=focus_absolute=200
